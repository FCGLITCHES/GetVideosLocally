# GetVideosLocally v3.2.8

## New status pill

- The plain "Connected to server" line under Download Now is now a compact status pill with a live signal indicator.
- When everything is working, the pill shrinks to a small icon with a steady waveform.
- Messages appear in the pill briefly and then clear on their own. Confirmations stay for 4 seconds, and warnings and errors for at most 7 seconds.
- The pill stays visible while the app is reconnecting to its server or your internet is down, and clears once you're back online.
- Warnings and errors use their own colours and icons, so you can see what went wrong at a glance.
- The pill respects reduced-motion settings.

## Downloads

Use the Windows installer to update an existing installation, or extract the portable ZIP and run GetVideosLocally.exe.

Completed downloads, history, and settings are preserved when upgrading.
