# GetVideosLocally v3.2.11

- **Your download choices now stick.** Format, quality and how many videos download at once stay as you set them, even after a restart.
- **New "Remember my last choices" switch** in the setup guide and in Settings → Downloads. Turn it off to start the same way every time.

Behind the scenes, settings are saved right away and the problem report is more accurate.

## Download

Use the installer to update, or unzip the portable version and run GetVideosLocally.exe. Your downloads, history and settings are kept.
