# GetVideosLocally v3.2.13

- **Now in 41 languages.** The app and the website speak Arabic, Hindi, Russian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and many more. Pick yours in **Settings → General → Appearance**.
- **Made for every script.** Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu read right to left, and every language gets a clear font made for its script.
- **The website picks your language by itself**, based on where you are or your device's language. You can change it with the globe button.

Behind the scenes, error messages and notifications from the app are translated too.

## Which file should I download?

- **Most people: `GetVideosLocally.Setup.3.2.13.exe`.** Open it to install the app. It then keeps itself up to date.
- **`GetVideosLocally-Portable-3.2.13.zip`** is for computers where you can't install apps, such as a work or school PC, or to run it from a USB stick. Unzip it and open `GetVideosLocally.exe`. It tells you when an update is out, but you download it yourself.
- You can ignore the other files. The app uses them to update.

Already on 3.2.12? The app updates itself: click **Restart to update** when you see it. Your downloads, history and settings are kept.
