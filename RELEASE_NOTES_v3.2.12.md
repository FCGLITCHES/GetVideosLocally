# GetVideosLocally v3.2.12

- **One-click updates.** New versions download by themselves. When you see **Restart to update**, click it. Your settings and history stay as they are.
- **New About page** in Settings, showing your version, with a **Check for updates** button.
- **Looks right on big screens.** Downloads and History make better use of large monitors, and the Speed limit setting is no longer squashed.

Behind the scenes, the app now keeps a backup copy of your settings.

## Download

This is the last update you need to install by hand. From now on, the installed app updates itself. The portable version tells you when a new one is out.

Your downloads, history and settings are kept when you upgrade.
